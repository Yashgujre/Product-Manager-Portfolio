The decision that drove this Lab

I shipped a Decision Log first because hiring managers do not need another animated prototype. They need to see judgment: how a tradeoff was framed, what was rejected, and how we would know if the call was right. Flash comes later. The artifact that actually travels across a team is the written decision.
